# QLine Vaults — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

QLine Vaults is provided by PC Repair Leeds LTD, trading as QLine IT.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The QLine Vaults browser extension and mobile app contain no
analytics, no telemetry and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, transmit
or store any information about you or your browsing.

## Where your data goes

The extension communicates only with the server address you configure — for
QLine staff, that is your employer's own QLine Vaults server. It sends data
nowhere else.

Your vault contents — passwords, notes, card details and attachments — are
encrypted on your own device before being sent to that server, using a key
derived from your master password. Your master password never leaves your
device and is never sent to the server. Nobody operating the server, including
QLine IT, can read your vault contents.

## What the server stores

Encrypted vault data, your email address, and security information necessary to
operate the service: the times you signed in, the devices you signed in from,
and the IP addresses those sign-ins came from. This is used to alert you to
sign-ins from new devices and to detect attacks. It is not used for any other
purpose and is not shared.

## Permissions

The extension can read and fill pages on any website. This is inherent to a
password manager: it cannot know in advance which sites you hold logins for. It
reads and writes only login and form fields, and only when you ask it to.

## Your rights

The vault is your employer's system where it is provided by an employer.
Requests to access or delete data should be made to your employer in the first
instance, or to QLine IT at the address below.
